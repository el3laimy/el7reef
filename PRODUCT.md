# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Tournament organizers, team captains, street players (both registered and guests), and their followers. They use this in the context of street football, often sharing results on WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose
To turn chaotic street tournaments into an official, professional experience. It's an "Ego MVP" where matches produce data, data produces pride, and pride produces sharing (shareable cards, top scorers, MVP).

## Brand Personality
The app name is **الحريف** in Arabic and **EL7REEF** in English. Proud, street-authentic, hype-driven, professional, and accessible. The tone is Egyptian/Arabic street football ("العب. اتوثق. اتفاخر.").

## Anti-references
Boring data-entry admin apps, generic SaaS tables, fake features ("coming soon"), and the chaos of WhatsApp groups. It should NOT look like a complex spreadsheet.

## Design Principles
- **Pride First:** Ego cards and achievements take precedence over raw data.
- **No Fake Surfaces:** Only real, working features that produce value.
- **Guests are First-Class:** Unregistered players get the full visual and emotional treatment.
- **One-Hand, Outdoor Context:** Actions must be easy to hit on a mobile device outdoors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Arabic RTL first, large legible typography (Cairo font), and strong contrast for outdoor/daylight visibility.
